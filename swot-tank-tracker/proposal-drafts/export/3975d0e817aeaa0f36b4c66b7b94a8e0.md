## SWOT limits and how to handle them

SWOT is not a perfect gauge: rain, calm water and hills can spoil a pass, so the project keeps only clean passes and leans on Sentinel-1 in the monsoon.

| Limit | What happens | How the project handles it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Heavy rain | SWOT's Ka-band radar is more sensitive to rain than the Ku-band radars on most earlier altimeters, and heavy rain degrades the data; the ocean product has a rain flag, the lake product has none ([AVISO](https://www.aviso.altimetry.fr/en/applications/atmosphere-wind-and-waves/wind-and-waves/winds-waves-and-rain-off-the-western-european-coasts-seen-by-swot-and-nadir-altimeters.html)) | Use quality flags and skip passes on heavy-rain days (from rainfall data); fit curves from dry-season passes; use Sentinel-1 for monsoon water area |
| Dark water | Calm, sheltered water reflects weakly; a known processing issue with a fix planned ([SWOT Science Team, 2024](https://swotst.aviso.altimetry.fr/fileadmin/user_upload/SWOTST2024/20240619/12_Wed_PM_Chen_HR_ReqStatPlan20240618u.pdf)) | Use quality flags; report affected tanks separately |
| Layover in hills | Nearly all land is in layover, though often with little impact ([SWOT Science Team, 2024](https://swotst.aviso.altimetry.fr/fileadmin/user_upload/SWOTST2024/20240619/12_Wed_PM_Chen_HR_ReqStatPlan20240618u.pdf)) | Prefer flat dry-zone cascades; treat hill reservoirs such as Victoria as a separate test |
| Specular ringing | Very bright reflections smear into nearby pixels | Use quality flags |
| Only listed lakes in Hydrocron | Ready-made time series exist only for lakes in SWOT's prior lake database ([NASA Earthdata](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/news/hydrocron-time-series-api-swot-hydrology-data-products-now-available)) | Check each tank's lake ID first; use the pixel cloud for tanks that are missing (stretch) |

## Ready-made starting points

The team does not start from zero: a pretrained water model, labelled tiles, a global storage dataset and official tank outlines already exist.

| Resource | What it gives | Use in the project |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Prithvi-EO-2.0 Sen1Floods11 model](https://huggingface.co/ibm-nasa-geospatial/Prithvi-EO-2.0-300M-TL-Sen1Floods11) | IBM and NASA water and flood segmentation model using six Sentinel-2 bands; Apache-2.0 licence | Starting model and baseline for the AI water map |
| [Sen1Floods11](https://github.com/cloudtostreet/Sen1Floods11) | 446 hand-labelled 512 × 512 tiles at 10 m, Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 | Pre-training |
| [GloLakes](https://essd.copernicus.org/articles/16/201/2024/) | Storage time series for more than 170,000 lakes (1984–2020), with near-real-time monitoring of 27,000; lakes over 1 km² only; volume from a terrain-slope model, not bathymetry | Comparison for the major reservoirs |
| UNDP tank outlines | Satellite-traced tank outlines used by [Lopane et al., 2025](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/10203133/1/Lopane_1-s2.0-S0301479725000076-main.pdf) | Tank inventory; request from UNDP |
| [Hydrocron](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/news/hydrocron-time-series-api-swot-hydrology-data-products-now-available) | SWOT time series as CSV or GeoJSON, by lake ID | SWOT water heights |
